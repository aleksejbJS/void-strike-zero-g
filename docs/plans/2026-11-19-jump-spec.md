# Jump and double jump with a forward flip — spec

Date: 2026-11-19 · Status: approved by owner 2026-11-19 · Branch: `feature/key-bindings` (owner: same
branch as key bindings, both ship in 0.5.1)

## Why

Variety: a second way to move next to the jetpack. Today there is no jump at all — W and Space both fire the
jetpack (`playerControl.ts` `wantsJump = Space || KeyW`); `JUMP_FORCE` / `DOUBLE_JUMP_FORCE` in
`constants.ts` are unused leftovers.

## Goals

- Space on the ground: a quick jump, about **1.5 fighter heights** (≈108 px for the 72 px standing fighter).
- Space in the air: **one** double jump per airtime adding about **1 fighter height** (≈72 px), with a
  **forward flip** of the fighter picture lasting **0.4 s**.
- Feels like the prototype the owner approved (`.superpowers/brainstorm/*/content/jump-prototype-v2.html`,
  local only): run, jump onto a 85 px shelf; jump + double jump onto a 150 px shelf.
- Everyone sees the flip, online and P2P.
- Key bindings get a "Jump" row (default Space); the jetpack keeps only W.

## Non-goals

- Bots jumping (they keep climbing with the jetpack) — separate work later.
- Achievements / stats for jumps, a jump sound, take-off dust.
- Jumping off the grappling hook.
- **Touch controls** (owner, 2026-11-19): no jump button on phones in this release; the move stick up stays
  the jetpack.

## Anti-goals

- The jetpack, rocket jumps or the general speed cap behaving differently.
- The flip giving any gameplay advantage beyond movement (no invulnerability, no hitbox change).
- Desync between the predicting client and the server.

## Approaches

Where the jump lives is fixed by the project: in the shared Force Pipeline (`movement.ts` via
`playerControl.ts`), which the server, the P2P host and client prediction all run. The choice was where the
**flip** lives:

- A — flip timer in the simulation, sent in snapshots. Frame-exact everywhere, but puts a cosmetic into the
  simulation (the project rule is that the simulation never writes cosmetics) and adds a per-tick field that
  changes nothing in play. Rejected.
- **B — flip drawn by each client (picked).** The simulation only records "the air jump of this airtime is
  used". The renderer starts a 0.4 s rotation when it sees that flag turn on; the flag stays on until
  landing, so a missed snapshot cannot skip a flip. Remote flips start with the usual remote delay.
- C — a network "flipped" event. Rejected: events can be lost or doubled under client prediction.

## Decided with the owner

### Jump rules

- Jump is a **press** (rising edge of the Jump input), not a hold. Holding Space does nothing more.
- On the ground: jump. Costs **no fuel**.
- In the air (walked off a ledge, after a jump, after the jetpack): the first press is the double jump,
  once per airtime, **no fuel**. Landing restores it.
- On the grappling hook: Space does nothing (W reels in, S lets the rope out, as today).
- The jetpack is W only and works exactly as today, also in the air between jumps.
- Lying (Z held) or crouching (S held): Space makes the fighter stand up and jump in one go (see "my calls").

### Flip

- Forward, in the direction of horizontal input at the moment of the double jump; with no A/D, the
  direction the fighter faces. One full turn in 0.4 s around the body centre.
- **Shooting stays allowed** during the flip (owner, after seeing both in the prototype): the body turns,
  the gun keeps pointing at the cursor and shots leave from the gun as usual.
- The hitbox does not change.

### Bindings

- New action `jump` (label "Jump" / "Прыжок"), standard input `Space`, default `['Space', null]`, listed
  right after the jetpack. Jetpack default becomes `['KeyW', null]`.
- The menu legend gains a "Space Jump" item; with WASD the jetpack item shows W.
- 0.5.1 is not released, so no stored layout needs migrating; `sanitizeBindings` already gives a missing
  action its defaults minus inputs in use.

## Constraints

- `movement.ts` clamps `vel.y` to ±`HARD_SPEED_CAP` (8). A jump strong enough for 1.5 heights starts faster
  than that, so the clamp must not cut a jump's rise, while the jetpack and rocket jumps keep today's cap.
- Heights are measured in the real simulation (Matter collision, fixed 120 ticks/s), not taken from the
  prototype's maths: tests assert the apex of a jump ≈ 1.5 × 72 px and of jump + double jump at the top
  ≈ 2.5 × 72 px (±10 %).
- The server already accepts `Space` in input messages (`validation.ts` ALLOWED_KEYS): no validation change.
- The Node online server runs the shared code. **The owner redeploys it on the day 0.5.1 ships**
  (owner, 2026-11-19), so no protocol version or fallback is built. Until then an online player pressing Space
  would be pulled back by the old server (it reads Space as jetpack); P2P hosts update with the page.
- Project rules: files ≤ 200 lines, named exports, no silent catches; en + ru strings.

## My calls (owner may override)

- **Lying / crouching:** Z and S are *held* keys, so a press that only stood the fighter up would do nothing
  while they stay held. Space therefore stands up and jumps at once (the existing rule "a jump cancels
  prone" extends to Space).
- **One-legged (wounded) fighter:** no jump and no double jump — it can only hop at crawl speed today.
- **Flag carrier (Hold the flag):** may jump, but the existing carrier slowdown (horizontal speed × 0.85 every
  tick, ground and air) stays as it is, so a carrier's jump goes nearly straight up. Carriers are meant to be
  slow; letting them leap onto ledges would change the mode's balance.
- **Throwing the flag** is "crouch + jump" today (S + W or S + Space). Both keep working: S + W and S + Space
  throw for a carrier, so the old habit stays; a crouching carrier's Space throws rather than jumps.
- **Gun during the flip:** the simulation fires from its fixed point above the feet and (approach B) knows
  nothing of the flip. So the gun is drawn **at that real shot point**, aimed at the cursor, and the body turns
  under it — shots visibly leave the gun. (The prototype let the shoulder travel round with the body; that
  would draw the gun up to ~28 px away from where bullets start.)
- **Flip direction** is stored with the air-jump flag (−1 / +1, set at the double jump from A/D or facing),
  because remote clients do not receive other players' keys.
- The new entity fields (jump held last tick, air jump used, its direction) go into the client's predicted
  fields (`PREDICTED_FIELDS` in `clientSession.ts`), otherwise each server snapshot would overwrite them and
  allow a second double jump.
- The jetpack's straight-up boost in `movement.ts` stays tied to W.
- The edge detection state ("jump held last tick") and "air jump used" are plain entity fields, so they travel
  in snapshots and client prediction reconciles them like any other field.
- Patch note 0.5.1 gets one more point: "Space jumps; a second Space in the air does a double jump with a
  forward flip — the jetpack is on W".

## Left to the builder

  jetpack/rocket behaviour hold).
- How the fighter rig rotates as a whole while the gun angle compensates so it still aims at the cursor.
- Where the flip timer lives on the client (per-fighter view state, like the landing squash in
  `fighterMotion.ts`).
